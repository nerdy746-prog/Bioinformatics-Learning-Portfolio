
# NCBI Gene Database Exploration

## Objective
To explore the NCBI database and understand how genetic information can be retrieved and analysed.

## Tool Used
NCBI (National Center for Biotechnology Information)

Website: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/

## Practical Work

- Investigated the PAH (Phenylalanine Hydroxylase) gene.
- Explored available gene information.
- Examined nucleotide sequence records.

## Learning Outcomes
- Understanding biological databases.
- Learning how to retrieve gene information.
- Understanding nucleotide sequence records.
